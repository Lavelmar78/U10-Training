# U10 Drill Library — Quick Guide for Coaches

**App link:** https://lavelmar78.github.io/U10-Training/

## Browsing drills

- Tap a category on the home screen (Warm-Up, Defending, Attacking, etc.) or **Browse All Drills**
- Tap any drill to see setup, coaching points, an animated pitch diagram, and a linked video where available

## Building a session

1. Tap **+** next to any drill to add it to your session
2. Tap **Session** (bottom of home screen) to see what you've added
3. Drag drills to reorder, or use the ▲▼ buttons
4. Tap **▶ Run** to go through the session with a live timer for each drill

## Joining the team

1. Tap **☁ Team** on the home screen
2. Enter the team code you were given (e.g. `FALCON-4821`) → **Join Team**
3. You'll stay signed in on this device — no need to re-enter the code each visit

*Don't have a code?* Ask whoever set up the team, or create a new team yourself if you're starting one from scratch.

## Involved with more than one team?

You can join as many teams as you like — just repeat the **Join a Team** step with each code. Under **☁ Team**, you'll see all your teams listed with the active one marked. Tap any other team to switch — sessions you save or view always apply to whichever team is currently active.

## Saving a session for the team

1. Build your session as above
2. Give it a **Session Name** (e.g. "Pre-season Week 1"), a **Session Date**, or both — you need at least one
3. Tap **☁ Save to Team**

Leave the date blank to save a reusable **template** (no fixed date) — handy for a session you'll run again later. Save as many sessions as you like.

## Viewing sessions other coaches have saved

1. **☁ Team → 📅 View Team Sessions**
2. Dated sessions are listed chronologically; templates appear at the end
3. Tap any one to load it back into the builder — review it, run it, or tweak and re-save it

## Sharing a session outside the team

On the Session screen, tap **⬆ Share** to copy a plain-text list of drills and durations — handy for pasting into WhatsApp or email.

---

**Tip:** the app works fully offline for browsing drills and building sessions — you only need internet for the Team features (saving/loading shared sessions).

## Add it to your Home Screen

Makes it open like a proper app, full screen, no browser bar.

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open the app link in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow) at the bottom
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open the app link in Chrome
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right)
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**
4. Tap **Add**, then **Add** again to confirm
